The Most Expensive Moment In A Business

Dear owner-director,

What's the most expensive moment in a business?

It's the hour you spend across the table from a professional buyer.

You win a bigger contract. Revenue goes up. The profit doesn't follow it in.

It comes down to that one room.

You're good at what you do. You earned the right to go after bigger clients and commercial contracts.

Now you're sitting across from procurement. A commercial manager. A main contractor's buyer. Someone who negotiates for a living.

They push on price. They keep coming back for more.

And because you want the job, you give. A little off the price. A little more scope. Net 30 turns into Net 60.

You keep the contract. You lose the margin, the cash flow, and your evenings.

It's not that you're soft. Nobody trained you for that room. It was never in your job description.

Well. It is now.

Get one big negotiation wrong and it costs you 10, 20, 30 thousand dollars before you start the work.

It's the negotiation game you didn't know you were playing.

Leave it to instinct, and this is where it goes:

  • You win bigger contracts and keep less of the money
  • You give ground just to keep the deal alive
  • A buyer compares you on price to a shop that does worse work
  • More work, tighter margin, more stress

The bigger the contract, the more this costs you.
